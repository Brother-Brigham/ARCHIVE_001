Part One: Tree of Knowledge

The means and setting of coming into relationship with God

1. Epistemology and revelation: 1 Ne 11; Moroni 10:3-5; D&C 8, 9, 50. Includes the operations of the Holy Ghost (testimony, revelation, sanctification), theophany, and reason and faith. Sources: Givens, Wrestling the Angel; Ostler, Exploring Mormon Thought.
2. Scripture and canon: Articles of Faith 8-9; the open canon; Bible formation and the standing of the Book of Mormon, D&C, and Pearl of Great Price. Foil: inerrancy and perspicuity (Grudem).
3. Authority and doctrine: the 2007 Newsroom statement "Approaching Mormon Doctrine"; the status of McConkie and the Journal of Discourses. Includes how this criterion relates to Part Two.
4. Creation and science: Moses 2-3; Abraham 4-5; D&C 93:29. Organization from existing matter, evolution, the 1909 and 1925 First Presidency statements. Points forward to Part Two ch. 2.
5. Anthropology and agency: 2 Ne 2:27; D&C 93:30-33; body and spirit.
6. The Fall: Moses 4-6; 2 Ne 2; Alma 12, 42. Article of Faith 2, the rejection of original sin and total depravity, and accountability (Moroni 8, D&C 68:25).
7. Satan, angels, and theodicy: Moses 4; D&C 29:36-38, 122; Rev 12. Source: Paulsen, "Joseph Smith and the Problem of Evil" (BYU Studies, 2007).
8. Gospel and order of salvation (the Atonement applied): 3 Ne 27; 2 Ne 25:23; Mosiah 4; Moroni 10:32. Faith, repentance, justification, sanctification, grace and works, assurance, enduring to the end, and calling and election made sure (D&C 131-132). Sources: Robinson, Believing Christ; Givens, Feeding the Flock. Cross-reference Part Two ch. 6.
9. Sin, repentance, and the unpardonable sin: Alma 36, 42; D&C 19, 58:42-43, 76:31-37; Heb 6.
10. Priesthood: D&C 84, 107. Keys, women and priesthood, and the race and priesthood ban (2013 Gospel Topics essay). Sources: Prince, Power from on High; Stapley, The Power of Godliness.
11. Ordinances and covenants (the instrument): baptism, confirmation, sacrament, ordination, healing, endowment, sealing, the new and everlasting covenant, laying on of hands. Note that the endowment carries content about God, so point forward to Part Two chs. 8-9. Sources: Stapley; Brown, In Heaven as It Is on Earth.
12. Dispensations, covenants, and Israel: Adamic, Noahic, Abrahamic, and Mosaic covenants; Abraham 2; 3 Ne 20-21; the gathering of Israel (Article of Faith 10). Foil: covenant theology.
13. Apostasy, Restoration, and the Church: Joseph Smith, the Joseph Smith Papers, organization, membership, and church discipline.
14. Marriage and family: D&C 131-132; the 1995 Proclamation; eternal and plural marriage; gender roles (foil: complementarianism). Sources: Hales, Joseph Smith's Polygamy; Compton, In Sacred Loneliness; the Gospel Topics essays. Cross-reference Part Two ch. 5.
15. Worship, practice, and ethics: prayer, fasting, Sabbath, spiritual gifts (1 Cor 12-14, Moroni 10), Word of Wisdom (D&C 89), tithing, consecration (D&C 104), Zion, citizenship (D&C 134, Article of Faith 12), and slavery (2 Ne 26:33). Source: Nibley, Approaching Zion.
16. Afterlife and salvation of the dead: Alma 40; D&C 76, 88, 137-138, 128; 1 Cor 15; 1 Pet 3-4. Spirit world, resurrection, judgment, kingdoms, and perdition. Foil: "no second chance."
17. Eschatology: D&C 29, 45, 88, 133; Matt 24. The Second Coming, Millennium, and New Jerusalem. Foil: amillennialism and premillennialism.

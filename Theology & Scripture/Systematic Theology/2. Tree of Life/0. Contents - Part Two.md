Part Two: Tree of Life

Who is known, and the relationship between God and humanity

1. Method and sources: Joseph Smith's developing theology; the four First Vision accounts; Moses and Abraham; Lectures on Faith (1835, removed 1921). Sources: Harrell, This Is My Doctrine; Joseph Smith Papers.
2. Creator-creature distinction: Moses 1; D&C 93:29; Abraham 3. Ex nihilo, monotheism, and the divine council (Ps 82; Mark S. Smith, Early History of God). Contrast: Paulsen on early Christian corporealism (Harvard Theological Review, 1990).
3. Attributes of God: D&C 130:22; Moses 7:28-37. Embodiment, omniscience, foreknowledge and providence, passibility, simplicity. Sources: Ostler vol. 1; Givens, The God Who Weeps.
4. The Godhead (ontology): Mosiah 15; D&C 130; Lectures on Faith 5. The Father, the Son, and the person and nature of the Holy Ghost, in comparison with the Trinity, perichoresis, and eternal generation. The Spirit's operations stay in Part One ch. 1.
5. Heavenly Mother: the 2015 Gospel Topics essay; Eliza R. Snow; Paulsen and Pulido, "A Mother There" (BYU Studies, 2011). Argue the strong version deductively from D&C 131:1-4 and 132:19-20, tagging each claim as revealed, inferred, or speculative. Cross-reference Part One ch. 14.
6. Christ: person and Atonement as revelation: premortal Jehovah; the incarnation (foil: the hypostatic union); Mosiah 3, 15; Alma 34; 2 Ne 9; D&C 19; Moses 7. Treats the Atonement as disclosing God's character. Cross-reference Part One ch. 8.
7. Premortality: Abraham 3:22-28; D&C 93:29, 138; Jer 1:5. Intelligences and the council in heaven. Source: Givens, When Souls Had Wings.
8. Humanity in God's image, heirs, and union with Christ: Gen 1:26-27; Rom 8:16-17; John 17; D&C 76:58; Moses 1:39 (God's glory).
9. Deification and exaltation (the covenant relationship realized): Irenaeus (Against Heresies 5, preface), Athanasius (On the Incarnation 54), John 10:34-36, 2 Pet 1:4, D&C 132:19-20. Source: Russell, The Doctrine of Deification in the Greek Patristic Tradition.
10. Exegesis of the King Follett Discourse (April 7, 1844): Larson, "A Newly Amalgamated Text" (BYU Studies, 1978); Hale, "Doctrinal Impact" (same issue); the Richards, Woodruff, Bullock, and Clayton reports; Ehat and Cook, Words of Joseph Smith.
11. Exegesis of the Sermon in the Grove (June 16, 1844): the same report set and the Joseph Smith Papers.

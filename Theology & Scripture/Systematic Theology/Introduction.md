Introduction: the organizing criterion

- Anchor texts: John 17:3, D&C 132:24, D&C 84:19-22, D&C 93:1.
- Principle: Part One treats the means and setting of coming to know God (instrumental). Part Two treats who is known and the relationship itself (constitutive).
- Admit in the introduction: "knowing" in John is relational, so Part Two is doctrine pointing toward knowing, not a substitute for it.
- Method: mark claims as revealed, inferred, or speculative throughout, especially in Part Two.

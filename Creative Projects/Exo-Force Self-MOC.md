**Ito** is an Exo-Force fighter pilot. Assigned to the secret division, he specializes in long-range combat, intelligence and espionage, and conducting solo operations deep behind enemy lines. Leaked documents indicate his pilot designation is **S.07**—the highest S designation currently known to the public, and indicating that Ito is, if not the seventh pilot, the seventh member of the secret division. Secret division designations are thought to be assigned per-member, rather than tied to the Battle Machine as with the air, land, water, and all terrain teams. 

---

## Personality

Cool, quiet, and methodically composed. Ito is the type of pilot who approaches combat as a series of calculated risks rather than emotional outbursts. While highly independent with a strong preference for solo long-patrol missions along the mountain ridges, he remains fiercely loyal to other Exo-Force pilots when assistance is needed. Pragmatic and cautious, Ito insists on carrying analog backup gear, adhering to the principle that a pilot must be prepared to survive ground-side if their battle machine falls. He is driven to do all he can to ensure as few humans as possible experience the kind of loss he has suffered at the hands of the robots.

---

## History

### Early Life & Recruitment
Prior to joining Exo-Force, Ito worked as an endurance test pilot operating high-altitude atmospheric haulers in the upper passes of Sentai Mountain. The dangerous, low-oxygen conditions made him adept at handling extreme altitude stress and unexpected mechanical failures mid-flight. 

His aptitude for handling such situations combined with a keen eye made him a shoo-in for Sentai Fortress’s budding intelligence unit—dubbed the “secret division”—when the first robot rebellion occurred. Ito tragically lost his wife, Luci (who was pregnant with their unborn daughter), to the two robots that had tended to their small family farm; he arrived home from a lengthy test flight to find her corpse in the kitchen, and the blood-stained robots had even waited for Ito to return before launching an unsuccessful attempt on his life. Earning the scar below his left eye in the skirmish, Ito tore the robots apart with his bare hands and swore to himself that no one he could help would have to go through what he did. He enlisted with Exo-Force the next day.

### Split Mountain Era
Following the uprising of Meca-One, Keiken and his closest advisors realized that information was everything. Forming a secret intelligence division, a small handful of elite pilots were selected and trained to operate uniquely modified battle machines for intelligence gathering and black operations. A select team of engineers, operating from a classified budget, developed these battle machines under such strict controls that few, if any, pilots and engineers of the main air and land teams would have even heard of the group.

Ito, assigned to the secret division, first began operating with a heavily modified *Stealth Hunter.* Following the development of the *Silent Strike* and Ryo’s shelved *Mountain Warrior* experiment, secret division engineers proposed that they follow the *Mountain Warrior* design philosophy: combining high armour with excellent maneuverability, extreme flight speed, and intense firepower. Building up a heavily armoured platform and incorporating innovations in stealth, weight management, and propulsion from the *Silent Strike*, the **Alpine Lance**, codenamed “Project Longinus”, was born.

### Golden City Era 
With the advent of the Golden City, Ito continued in his secret division capacity, gathering critical intelligence and conducting various missions deep in the robot-controlled side of Sentai Mountain. The discovery of the *Aero Booster* in the Golden City’s computers provided Ito’s engineering team a few new ideas for improving the altitude and speed capabilities of the **Alpine Lance**. The primary modifications entailed the development of a new, large, back-mounted propulsion system, allowing for enhanced, heavier armour and more intensive armaments to be incorporated in the **Alpine Lance Mk. II** design. 

Further mission details in this era remain classified. Leaked documents are heavily redacted, to the point of holding little usable information, though one leaked engineering document leads some to believe that the **Alpine Lance Mk. II** was built from scratch, rather than by modifying the **Alpine Lance**, in order to accommodate all of the new systems and sensor arrays.

### Deep Jungle Era
All secret division activities in the Deep Jungle era remain heavily classified. Many speculate that they have shifted to retaking the robot-controlled portions of the mountain while Meca-One is in the jungles below, others wonder if they have continued to assist in Sensei Keiken’s rescue through a clandestine capacity, and some question whether the secret division continues to operate at all.

---

## History

Created under a classified budget authorized by Sensei Keiken and his high council, the initial *Alpine Lance* was designed to solve a core tactical dilemma: traditional heavy assault machines were too slow for deep-reconnaissance, while light air interceptors lacked the armour to survive heavy robot anti-air fire.

By stripping down Ryo's shelved *Mountain Warrior* blueprint and rebuilding it with lightweight, high-tensile composite alloys developed for the *Silent Strike*, engineers created an armored fortress frame capable of extreme flight speeds and near-zero electronic visibility. Discovery of Golden City technologies allowed for a complete rebuild with new and enhanced armour materials, an *Aero Booster*-inspired propulsion system, a more reliable power supply, and a fully redesigned, bleeding-edge suite of sensor arrays and technology packages that make the **Alpine Lance Mk. II** the most advanced Battle Machine of the secret division’s air-superiority stealth lineup.

All known information about the capabilities of the **Alpine Lance Mk. II** comes from leaked classified documents.

## Information
*(Based on the modern F-35 Lightning II’s technology, as described at https://www.jsf.mil/f35tech.)*
* **Sensor Packages:**
	* **Advanced Electro-Optical Targeting System**: The Electro-Optical Targeting System (EOTS) is an affordable, high-performance, lightweight, multi-function system that provides precision air-to-air and air-to-surface targeting capability. The low-drag, stealthy EOTS is integrated into the chassis with a durable sapphire window and is linked to the aircraft’s integrated central computer through a high-speed fiber-optic interface. As the first sensor to combine forward-looking infrared and infrared search and track functionality, EOTS enhances pilots’ situational awareness and allows aircrews to identify areas of interest, perform reconnaissance and precisely deliver laser and GPS-guided weapons.
	* **Distributed Aperture System**: The only 360 degree, spherical situational awareness system. The DAS surrounds the aircraft with a protective sphere of situational awareness. It warns the pilot of incoming battle machines and missile threats as well as providing day/night vision, fire control capability and precision tracking of wingmen/friendly battle machines for increased tactical maneuvering. 
		* The DAS provides:
			- Missile detection and tracking
			- Launch point detection
			- Situational awareness Infrared Search and Track System (IRST) & cueing
			- Weapons support
			- Day/night navigation
	* **Helmet Mounted Display System**: In lieu of a physical Head-up Display (HUD), the Alpine uses a Helmet-mounted Display (HMD). The Alpine is the first modern battle machine to use an HMD to the exclusion of a HUD. The HMD projects two identical images onto the visor, one for each eye, focused at infinity. HUD vector symbology as well as sensor video is projected onto the visor. One of the most interesting sensors on the aircraft is the Distributed Aperture System (DAS). Surrounding the aircraft are 6 staring infrared cameras which are sensitive to thermal radiation. The video processing computer seamlessly stitches the cockpit individual images together into a 4π steradian sphere for the pilot to look through. As the pilot positions the helmet line of sight the appropriate portion of the imaged sphere is projected onto the visor. This makes it possible to “look through the aircraft structure”. Because the cameras are located external to the cockpit pilots have remarked that “it is like flying a glass airplane.” This capability is extremely useful when trying to position the battle machine from a hover. The next-generation user interface serves as the pilot’s primary display system, and virtual capabilities enable them to see through the bottom of the battle machine or directly at a target. With an uninterrupted display of flight information and sensor data, the pilot experiences extreme spatial orientation, superior weapons targeting, and tactical superiority–both day and night.​
	* **Active Electronically Scanned Array**: The Alpine’s radar system has an active, electronically scanned multi-function array (MFA) and the RF support electronics necessary to support a fully functional radar. It also has integrated radar software modes that are hosted on the integrated core processor. The radar operates through the top-mounted radar assembly, which has a wide bandwidth, enabling high-power transmissions over a large frequency range.
	* **Autonomic Logistics Information System**: ALIS turns maintenance data into actionable information that enables pilots, maintainers and military leaders to make proactive decisions and keep battle machines in the fight.
	* **Operational Data Integrated Network**: ODIN will be a cloud-native system that incorporates a new integrated data environment and a new suite of user-centered applications; it will be a significant step forward to improve sustainment and readiness performance. ODIN will be designed to substantially decrease administrator and maintainer workload, increase mission capability rates, and allow software engineers to rapidly develop and deploy updates in response to emerging warfighter requirements.
	* **Electronic Warfare Suite**: The EWS protects the Alpine with advanced technology for next generation missions to counter current and emerging threats. Equipped with offensive and defensive electronic warfare options for the pilot and aircraft, the suite provides fully integrated radar warning, targeting support, and self-protection, to detect and defeat surface and airborne threats.The system provides the pilot with maximum situational awareness, helping to identify, monitor, analyze, and respond to potential threats. Advanced avionics and sensors provide a real-time, 360-degree view of the battlespace, helping to maximize detection ranges and provide the pilot with options to evade, engage, counter or jam threats. Always active, EWS provides all-aspect, broadband protection, allowing the Alpine to reach well-defended targets and suppress enemy radars. The system operates in signal-dense environments, providing the battle machine with radio-frequency and infrared countermeasures, and rapid response capabilities. EWS is a platform-level solution that provides the Alpine with improved reliability and maintainability, helping reduce long term life cycle costs in keeping the battle machine fielded now and into the future.

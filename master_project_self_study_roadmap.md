  - Build the dedicated section/template on your site to house architecture diagrams and project write-ups.

  - Export Substack archive, convert to clean Markdown, configure RSS/redirects, and publish on your own domain.

  - Draft a structured horizon-scanning memo on 5-year security and ethical implications of one emerging tech *(USU CAI 6300 / CAI 6400 prep)*.

  - Finalize publishing workflows, site readiness, and research templates ahead of mid-December nonprofit approval.
- [ ] **Write documentation for existing home lab projects** `~1–2 weeks` #homelab #writing
  - Document hardware/VM topology, setup steps, troubleshooting logs, and security hardening decisions.

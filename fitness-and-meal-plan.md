# Fitness & Meal Plan

## Goals
- Lose about 1 lb/week with a gentle deficit
- Protect joints (lower back, knees, ankles, wrists, shoulders, neck)
- Calories: **2,100-2,300/day** | Protein: **140-160 g/day**
- Weigh in once a week. Losing over 2 lbs/week for several weeks: eat a bit more. No change after 3 weeks: trim about 150 calories.

# Part 1: Workout Routine

## Weekly Schedule
| Day | Activity |
|---|---|
| Mon | Workout A |
| Tue | Walk 20-30 min |
| Wed | Workout B |
| Thu | Walk 20-30 min |
| Fri | Workout A |
| Sat | Longer walk, 30-45 min |
| Sun | Rest |

Alternate each week: Week 1 is A / B / A, Week 2 is B / A / B (swap which workout falls on Mon, Wed, Fri).

## Warm-up (every strength day, ~7 min)
- [ ] 5 min easy walk
- [ ] Wall slides: 2x10
- [ ] Bird dogs: 1x6 per side

## Workout A
1. Chair box squat: 3x8-12
2. Incline push-up (hands on counter, fists or neutral wrists): 3x8-12
3. Glute bridge: 3x12-15
4. Inverted row (sturdy table): 3x6-10
5. Dead bug: 3x6-8 per side
6. Side plank (knees bent is fine): 2x20-30 sec per side

## Workout B
1. Supported split squat (hold a counter, shallow depth): 3x8-10 per leg
2. Incline push-up with 3-second lowering phase: 3x6-10
3. Single-leg glute bridge (or two-leg with a 3-sec hold): 3x8-10 per side
4. Prone Y-raise on the floor (no weight, thumbs up): 3x8-10
5. Bird dog (2-sec pause at top): 3x8 per side
6. Forearm plank: 3x15-30 sec

## Rest and Effort
- Rest 60-90 sec between sets
- Stop each set 1-2 reps short of failure
- Slow, controlled movement

## Progression
- Hit the top of the rep range on all sets for 2 sessions in a row, then make it harder: lower surface for push-ups, lower chair or deeper range for squats, longer plank holds.
- Add 5 min to walks every 1-2 weeks.
- After 6-8 weeks, if back and knees feel solid, consider a kettlebell for goblet squats and light hinges.

## Optional Mobility (10-15 min, 2-3x/week, on walk days or after strength)
- Cat-cow: 8-10 slow reps
- Child's pose: 30-60 sec
- Supine knee-to-chest: 30 sec per side
- Supine figure-4 glute stretch: 30 sec per side
- Hip flexor stretch (half-kneeling on a cushion): 30 sec per side
- Doorway chest stretch, arms below shoulder height: 30 sec
- Gentle neck tilts: 20 sec per side
- Wrist circles and gentle forearm stretches

**Avoid for now:** deep forward folds, deep twists, pigeon pose, downward dog. Choose gentle, chair, or restorative yoga over vinyasa or power.

## Safety Rules
- Mild discomfort (3/10 or less) that fades within a day is OK.
- Sharp pain, pain lingering into the next day, or numbness/tingling means stop and swap in an easier move.
- Test the table with your body weight before inverted rows. If it shifts, skip them and do Y-raises twice.
- Skip for now: jump rope, box jumps, burpees, sprints, sit-ups, Russian twists, twisting leg raises, dips, overhead and handstand work.
- Consider one visit with a doctor or physical therapist, especially for the heel pain.

# Part 2: 7-Day Meal Plan (~$50/week budget, for two)

Portions below are for me. My wife eats the same foods in the amounts her dietitian recommends (PCOS plan takes priority).

## Batch-Cook Days
- **Sunday (~90 min):**
  - [ ] Bake 2 trays chicken leg quarters (425°F, 45-50 min, salt and pepper)
  - [ ] Big pot of lentils
  - [ ] Big pot of rice
  - [ ] Bake 6-8 potatoes
- **Wednesday (~60 min):**
  - [ ] Pot of beans (or bean-cabbage-carrot soup)
  - [ ] Another pot of rice
  - [ ] Bake remaining chicken
- Cooked chicken and rice keep 3-4 days in the fridge. Freeze extras in single portions.
- If hungry, add rice or oats. If gaining weight, trim them.

## Sunday
- **Breakfast:** 3 scrambled eggs, 1 cup oats with a spoon of PB
- **Lunch:** Lentils over rice, 1 chicken quarter
- **Dinner:** 2 chicken quarters, baked potato, frozen veg
- **Snack:** 1 cup yogurt, banana

## Monday
- **Breakfast:** Oats with PB and banana, glass of milk
- **Lunch:** Lentil soup with rice, 1 can tuna
- **Dinner:** 2 chicken quarters, potato, frozen broccoli
- **Snack:** 1 cup yogurt

## Tuesday
- **Breakfast:** 3 eggs, banana, oats
- **Lunch:** Shredded chicken, rice, cabbage-carrot slaw (salt, pepper, splash of vinegar)
- **Dinner:** Lentil and potato stew with 1 chicken quarter
- **Snack:** Boiled egg, milk

## Wednesday (batch day)
- **Breakfast:** Oats with PB, banana
- **Lunch:** Chicken, rice, frozen veg
- **Dinner:** Bean and cabbage soup, 2 chicken quarters
- **Snack:** 1 cup yogurt

## Thursday
- **Breakfast:** 3 eggs with potato hash (leftover baked potato, fried)
- **Lunch:** Beans and rice with 1 can tuna
- **Dinner:** 2 chicken quarters, rice, carrots
- **Snack:** PB on banana

## Friday
- **Breakfast:** Oats, milk, banana
- **Lunch:** Tuna-potato mash patties, cabbage slaw
- **Dinner:** Chicken and lentil rice bowl with frozen veg, 2 chicken quarters
- **Snack:** Yogurt

## Saturday
- **Breakfast:** 3-egg omelet with veg
- **Lunch:** Beans, rice, 1 chicken quarter, can of tuna
- **Dinner:** Clean-out soup (leftover beans, veg, potato, chicken)
- **Snack:** Boiled egg, milk

# Part 3: Weekly Grocery List (~$50-60, prices vary)

- [ ] Chicken leg quarters, 10 lb bag (~$8-12)
- [ ] Eggs, 2 dozen (~$7)
- [ ] Dried lentils and beans, 3 lb (~$5)
- [ ] Rice, 5 lb (~$4)
- [ ] Oats (~$4)
- [ ] Potatoes, 5-10 lb (~$5)
- [ ] Frozen vegetables, 4 bags (~$6)
- [ ] Cabbage, carrots, bananas (~$6)
- [ ] Peanut butter (~$3)
- [ ] Canned tuna, 4 cans (~$4)
- [ ] Milk or plain yogurt (~$5)

**Staples to stretch it:** salt, pepper, garlic powder, vinegar (a $2 spice jar goes a long way).

## If budget reaches $70-120, add
- Second tub of yogurt, onions, spinach
- Fresh produce variety (peppers, apples, berries)
- Cottage cheese, canned sardines, extra eggs
- Olive oil, spices, sauces
- Whole-grain bread or tortillas, frozen fish

## Money-Saving Notes
- Buy family-size packs and freeze in portions.
- Buy store brands, shop sales, check clearance meat.
- Drink water and coffee instead of soda or juice.
- Check SNAP eligibility and local food pantries.
- Show this menu to my wife's dietitian early so they can adjust carb-heavy items for her.
